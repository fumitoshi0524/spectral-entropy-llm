Reading the evidence

A lower effective rank does not guarantee better language modeling. Large gains against naive truncation can coexist with high absolute perplexity. The strongest method varies with model scale and retained rank; compare the full curves rather than a single improvement factor.

Scope and source records

These are historical results. The adapted weights were not retained. The activation-aware curve is this repository’s whitening-based implementation, not the complete SVD-LLM pipeline. A full truncation curve for the matched LM-only adapted baseline is not archived, limiting causal attribution specifically to entropy regularization. Memory and speed were not measured.
